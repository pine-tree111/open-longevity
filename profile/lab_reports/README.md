# Raw Lab Reports Drop-Zone

Drop your blood test PDFs, hospital slips, or photo scans (PNG, JPG, PDF) into this folder.

### Privacy Assurance
Everything in this folder is strictly ignored by Git. Your medical documents will never be tracked, staged, or uploaded to GitHub.

### AI Parsing
Once you drop a file here, you can prompt your AI agent:
"I dropped a new lab report in profile/lab_reports/. Please inspect it, extract all biomarker values and units, and update my profile/user_profile.yaml."
